法拉第电磁感应定律 (Faraday's Law Simulation)

磁铁控制 (Magnet Controls)

线圈控制 (Coil Controls)

显示选项 (Display Options)

说明 (Instructions): 移动磁铁靠近或远离线圈会产生感应电流。电压表(电压)测量感应电动势(EMF)，灯泡会在有电流时发光。白色曲线表示磁感线。

The voltmeter (电压) measures the induced EMF generated as the magnetic field through the coil changes. The bulb lights up when current flows. White lines show magnetic field lines.
